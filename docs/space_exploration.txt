Space exploration has expanded humanity's understanding of the universe. Scientists use telescopes, satellites, and spacecraft to observe distant planets, stars, and galaxies. Robotic missions collect valuable scientific data while astronauts perform experiments aboard space stations.

Modern space agencies develop reusable rockets to reduce the cost of launching missions. Engineers design spacecraft capable of surviving harsh conditions such as radiation, extreme temperatures, and the vacuum of space. Future missions may establish permanent bases on the Moon before sending humans to Mars.

Space exploration encourages international cooperation and technological innovation. Research conducted for space missions often produces advances that benefit medicine, communication, transportation, and computer science on Earth.
